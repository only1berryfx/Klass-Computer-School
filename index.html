ABOUT KLASS

More Than Just A Computer School

Klass Computer School is a practical technology training centre dedicated to helping individuals develop useful digital and computer skills for today's world.

We believe that technology becomes more valuable when people know how to use it confidently. That is why our training focuses on practical learning, hands-on exercises and skills that can be applied in school, the workplace, business and everyday life.

Whether you are completely new to computers, looking to improve your office skills or ready to enter the world of programming and web development, Klass provides a friendly environment where you can learn, practise and grow.
